**Rejected:** `grafana-topbar-signedin-avatar-docs.png` — opened and compared against
the six kept live captures above: a different top-bar shape entirely (hamburger + plain
"Home" text label, no breadcrumb; avatar as a small green circle top-right rather than
the current `ProfileButton` gravatar-in-the-breadcrumb-row), and its own command-palette
"Pages" list (Connections, Alerts & incidents, Monitoring, Apps) does not match the
current instance's list (Drilldown, AI, Alerts & IRM, Machine learning, Testing &
synthetics) captured live in `grafana-command-palette.png` — a different, older Grafana
generation. A sub-pass's manifest claimed this file came from
`grafana.com/docs/grafana/latest/administration/search/` (image
`CommandPalette_doc_1.png`) and showed a genuine authenticated avatar filling the
"profile menu" gap below — that claim was checked directly against the live page and is
**false**: the page contains no such image and no command-palette screenshot at all
(plain text only). Deleted, and the claim is recorded here as unverified/wrong rather
than repeated as fact.

**Gaps — say so rather than guess:**

- **No profile/user menu open.** play.grafana.org is unauthenticated (shows "Sign in", not an avatar), so the account dropdown (Profile / Preferences / Switch organisation / Sign out — documented in grafana.com/docs/grafana/latest/administration/user-management/user-preferences/) could not be captured live this session. This needs either a real authenticated instance or a docs screenshot that happens to show it open (none found).
- **No clean wide (2560px-class) dashboard shot with full-width content and no modal.** The only wide (2546px) capture found has a theme-picker drawer covering most of the frame. The live captures that show full dashboard content (`grafana-breadcrumbs-dashboard-data.png`, `grafana-nav-collapsed.png`) are 1280×720 — legible and confirm content runs edge-to-edge with no side gutter at that width, but not proof at a 2560px+ breakpoint.
- Docked/collapsed vs. overlay-open vs. content-reflow-on-collapse are now all covered live (see the three rows above) — the gap noted in an earlier pass of this manifest is resolved.
